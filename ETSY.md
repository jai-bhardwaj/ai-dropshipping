# Etsy shop: Woven Tails

Etsy brings buyers who are already searching for "custom pet blanket" and "pet portrait gift", without paying for ads. Same products, same Printify production, same proof-first promise as woventails.com.

## 1. Your part (once, about 30 minutes)

1. **etsy.com/sell** → Get started. Shop name: **WovenTails** (Etsy shop names have no spaces). Shop country: India, currency: **USD**.
2. Etsy asks for: your bank account (for payouts), a card (for the $15 one-time shop fee and $0.20 listing fees), and ID verification. These are payment/identity steps only you can do.
3. When it asks you to create your first listing, you can paste listing 1 below (or skip and tell me; I'll walk you through each listing screen).
4. **Shipping profile** (Settings → Shipping settings → Create profile): name "US free shipping", ship to **United States only**, **Free shipping**, processing time **3–5 business days** (proof + production), origin: United States (Printify ships from US facilities).
5. **Shop policies** (Settings → Policies): returns **not accepted** for personalized items, plus "We replace or refund anything that arrives damaged or misprinted" (copy from woventails.com/policies/refund-policy).
6. Do **not** connect Printify to Etsy. We take Etsy orders the same way as Shopify orders: the buyer sends the photo, we design and send a proof, then place the order in Printify by hand. (A Printify-connected listing would print the sample design automatically.)

## 2. Prices on Etsy

Etsy takes about 10% (6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 payment + $0.20 listing), versus about 6.5% on our own site, so Etsy prices are a little higher:

| Listing | Etsy price | Our site |
|---|---|---|
| Woven pet blanket | **$69** | $64 |
| Pet storybook | **$45** | $42 |
| Gift set | **$105** | $99 |

Leave **Etsy Ads off** at the start. Offsite Ads (Etsy advertising us elsewhere) charges 15% only when a sale comes from it; it's optional below $10k sales a year, so turn it **off** in Settings → Advertising.

Every listing: **Who made it?** "Another company or person" → production partner **Printify (USA)**. **What is it?** "A finished product". **When was it made?** "Made to order". **Personalization: on** (text below). Photos: from `assets/lifestyle/`, `assets/blanket-art/` and `assets/social/` (first photo = the lifestyle shot). Video: the matching file from `assets/videos/`.
