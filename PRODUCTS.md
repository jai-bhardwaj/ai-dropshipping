## Why these products (short version)

The first shortlist (hand warmers, photo printer, motion lights) failed because Amazon sells the same items for $8–20 (see `PRICING_RESEARCH.md`). The second round checked **10 personalized products** against hard rules:
1. Our price ≤ 1.3× the cheapest comparable listing on Amazon/Etsy/Walmart
2. ≥ $20 margin per order
3. $30–70 price · 4. easy to demo, giftable · 5. safe, no IP/health claims · 6. US delivery ≤ 8 business days

## Product details (from Printify catalog, live 2026-10-08)

### Woven blanket: Printify blueprint 1626
- **Material:** 100% cotton, woven (jacquard) threads, colorful fringed edges
- **Variant:** choose **"Artwork"** (bold color blocks; best for AI illustrations). Raw photos weave muddy, which is exactly why we sell an illustrated portrait.
- **Size we sell:** 52×37" ($28.85 at Printify Choice). Larger sizes cost too much to keep the price competitive (60×50" would leave ~$16 margin).
- **Size tolerance:** ±2.5" (say "approx. 52×37 in" on the page)
- **Production:** ~1.2 days average (Printify Choice); **shipping** 2–5 business days → **~4–7 business days total**
- **Shipping cost:** $10.39 first item, +$4.99 each additional blanket

### Storybook: Printify blueprint 2737 (Hardcover Photo Book)
- **Size:** 8×8", 24 pages, case-bound hardcover (98pt chipboard), glossy or matte laminated cover
- **Printing:** full-bleed, no gutter, each page uploaded separately (empty pages print blank)
- **Paper:** Printify lists both "100 lb uncoated" and "semi-gloss archival". Check on the first order.
- **Production:** ~1.5 days (Printify Choice); **shipping** 2–5 business days
- **Shipping cost:** $6.19 first item, +$2.40 each additional

### Christmas cutoff
Printify providers list up to 10 days maximum handling in peak season. Set the **Christmas order deadline to December 10** (standard shipping) and say so on every page.

## Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Cheaper "woven" look-alikes on Amazon | Checked: the $23.45 Tidal Colors listing is a dye-sublimation printed picnic blanket, not jacquard-woven cotton (`VERIFICATION.md`). Keep watching for real cotton jacquard under $49 |
| Woven detail loss | Only bold, simple AI styles (see `OPERATIONS.md`); proof approval before printing |
| Storybook takes 1–2 hours per order | Fixed story template with name/species swaps (in `OPERATIONS.md`); batch orders daily |
| Character consistency across book pages | Generate one character reference first, reuse it for every page |
| Sample cost to India | Book sample $26.01 (~₹2,300, 10–30 business days); blanket $49.64 is out of budget, so the first blanket order is the quality check (free replacement if anything is off) |
| Customers' pet photos | Used only for their order, deleted after 90 days, never in ads without written permission (in `POLICIES.md`) |
