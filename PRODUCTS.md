## AI UGC rules (protect your ad accounts)

- **Don't present the AI person as a real customer** with fake experiences ("I've used this for 3 months, it changed my life"). In the US, fake or AI-generated testimonials are illegal (FTC rule on fake reviews), and Meta/YouTube ban accounts for it.
- **Do use the "presenter/demo" style:** "Here's how this works", "3 reasons this is the gift of the year", "watch what happens when I open this cabinet".
- **Label AI content where the platform asks:** Meta and YouTube have an "AI-generated / altered content" setting. Turn it on.
- **Show the real product accurately:** use real product clips (from the supplier or your sample) in every video; no AI-made features the product doesn't have.
- **No made-up numbers:** no "50,000 sold" or "#1 rated" unless it's true for your store.
