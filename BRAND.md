## Positioning
**One line:** Your pet, turned into art you can hold: illustrated keepsakes made from your photo.

**Who it's for:** pet parents (mostly women 25–55) who treat their dog or cat like family, and people buying a heartfelt gift for them (partners, kids buying for parents, friends after a pet passes).

**Why us (true claims only):**
- Illustrated from **your own photo**: not a stock breed picture
- **You approve the artwork** before anything is made
- Woven from **100% cotton** / printed **hardcover**, made and shipped in the US
- Free replacement if anything's wrong

**Tone of voice:** warm, playful, never cheesy; short sentences; pet puns used sparingly (max one per piece).
