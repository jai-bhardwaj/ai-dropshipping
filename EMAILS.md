# Email flows: Shopify Email or Klaviyo (free plans)

Placeholders: [STORE NAME], [SUPPORT EMAIL], [PRODUCT], [DISCOUNT CODE], [DATE].
Tone: warm, short, US English. Every marketing email needs an unsubscribe link (both tools add it automatically) and your business address in the footer.

Set up in this order (most money first): **1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6**.

---

## 1. Abandoned checkout (Shopify: Marketing → Automations → "Recover abandoned checkout")

**Email 1: 1 hour after**
- Subject: You left something behind
- Preview: Your cart is saved, finish in one click
- Body:
  > Hi [first name],
  >
  > Looks like you didn't finish your order. No worries, we saved your cart.
  >
  > [Product image + name + price]
  >
  > **[Complete my order →]**
  >
  > Questions about sizing, shipping or anything else? Just reply to this email; a real person reads it.
  >
  > [STORE NAME]

**Email 2: 24 hours after**
- Subject: Still thinking it over?
- Body:
  > Hi [first name],
  >
  > Your [PRODUCT] is still in your cart. A few things people usually ask:
  > - **Shipping:** [PROCESSING + DELIVERY] business days, with tracking
  > - **Returns:** [30-day returns / free replacement if anything's wrong]
  > - **Holiday delivery:** order by [DATE] for Christmas
  >
  > **[Finish my order →]**

**Email 3: 48 hours after** (only if margin allows a discount)
- Subject: 10% off, just for you
- Body:
  > Hi [first name],
  >
  > Here's 10% off your order, valid for 48 hours: **[DISCOUNT CODE]**
  >
  > **[Use my 10% off →]**
- Note: make the code single-use per customer and set a real 48-hour expiry in Shopify Discounts (don't fake the deadline).

---

## 2. Welcome series (trigger: email signup via popup/footer)

**Popup offer:** "Get 10% off your first order" (Shopify Forms app, free). Show after 8 seconds or on exit; mobile-friendly.

**Email 1: immediately**
- Subject: Welcome! Here's your 10% off
- Body:
  > Welcome to [STORE NAME]!
  >
  > Here's your code: **[WELCOME10]** (10% off your first order).
  >
  > [1 line about what the store is: e.g. "Gifts that are made just for the people (and pets) you love."]
  >
  > **[Shop now →]**

**Email 2: 2 days later**
- Subject: Our most-loved [gift/product]
- Body: best-selling product with 3 bullet benefits + 1 real customer review (only once you have real ones) + button.

**Email 3: 4 days later**
- Subject: Your 10% off ends soon
- Body: reminder that the code expires in 48 hours (set a real expiry) + button.

---

## 3. Order confirmation (Shopify: Settings → Notifications → Order confirmation)

Add this text above the order summary:
> Thanks for your order, [first name]! 🎉
>
> **What happens next:**
> 1. We're preparing your order now (takes [PROCESSING DAYS] business days).
> 2. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.
> 3. Delivery usually takes [DELIVERY DAYS] business days after shipping.
>
> [For personalized orders: "We'll email you if we need anything for your design."]
>
> Questions? Just reply to this email.

---

## 4. Shipping confirmation (Settings → Notifications → Shipping confirmation)

> Good news: your order is on its way! 📦
>
> Track it here: [tracking link]
>
> Tracking can take 24–48 hours to show the first update. Packages may ship in more than one box.

---

## 5. Review request (Judge.me, automatic)

- Send: **7 days after delivery** (Judge.me → Settings → Review request timing → after fulfillment + delivery days)
- Subject: How's your [PRODUCT]?
- Body:
  > Hi [first name],
  >
  > We hope you love your [PRODUCT]! Would you take 30 seconds to tell us what you think? A photo helps other shoppers a lot.
  >
  > [⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ review buttons]
  >
  > If anything's not right, reply to this email first. We'll fix it.
- **Don't** offer discounts only for positive reviews; offering a reward is OK only if it's for any honest review and disclosed.

---

## 6. Post-purchase / cross-sell (trigger: 14 days after delivery)

- Subject: A little something for your next gift
- Body:
  > Hi [first name],
  >
  > Thanks again for shopping with us. Holidays are coming up, so here's **15% off** your next order: **[THANKYOU15]** (valid 14 days).
  >
  > [2–3 product tiles: the other products / bundle]

## Holiday campaigns (one-off emails to your list)

| Send date | Subject | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 20 | Early Black Friday access for you | Offer starts early for subscribers |
| Nov 27 (Black Friday) | Our biggest offer of the year is live | BF offer + ending date |
| Nov 30 (Cyber Monday) | Last chance: Cyber Monday ends tonight | Final reminder |
| [Christmas cutoff − 3 days] | 3 days left to get it before Christmas | Shipping deadline |
| [Christmas cutoff] | Last day for Christmas delivery | Final deadline |

All deadlines and discount end dates must be **real**.
