Video kit · YouTube Shorts + Pinterest

11 videos, one per day. Upload them once and each platform posts them on schedule. Same videos, same captions.

YouTube Shorts (about 15 minutes)

  1. Create a YouTube channel named Woven Tails (youtube.com → your avatar → Create a channel; handle @woventails).
  2. On a computer open studio.youtube.com → Create → Upload videos. For each video below: Download video, drop it in, paste the caption as the title (first line) and description (all of it), and add woventails.com to the description.
  3. Audience: No, it's not made for kids. Under "Altered or synthetic content" choose Yes (the pets are AI illustrations).
  4. Visibility: Schedule. Day 1 = tomorrow 7:00 pm US Eastern, Day 2 the day after, and so on. Vertical videos under 60 s become Shorts automatically.

Pinterest (about 10 minutes)

  1. Create a free Pinterest business account (pinterest.com/business/create) named Woven Tails, website woventails.com.
  2. Create a board Custom Pet Blankets & Gifts.
  3. For each video: Create → Pin, upload it, paste the caption, link https://woventails.com, then Publish at a later date (one per day).
