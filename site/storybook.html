Questions

About the book.

Will the pet look like mine?

It's a picture-book illustration that keeps your pet's real colors, markings and features. You approve the character before we illustrate the book.

Is the art made with AI?

Yes, AI-assisted illustration, checked by hand against your photo.

Can I change the story?

The story is the same for every book; the pet, names, favorite treat and version (dog, cat or memorial) are personalized.

Can I include two pets?

Not yet. One pet per book for now.

Can I return it?

It's made just for you, so we can't accept change-of-mind returns. Any printing issue gets a free reprint.

When will it arrive?

About 5–8 business days after you approve the character. Order by Dec 10 for Christmas.
