No. Upload one photo in the designer above. We use it for the blanket and for the storybook character, and you approve both in one email.

Yes: Bold Pop, Christmas, Royal or Memorial. Pick it when you design the blanket.

Both pieces are made to order, so no change-of-mind returns. Anything wrong gets a free replacement or refund.
