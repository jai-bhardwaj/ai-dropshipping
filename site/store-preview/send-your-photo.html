Send your pet's photo

Ordered without a photo, or want to send a better one?

  1. Reply to your order confirmation email, or email hello@woventails.com with your order number (for example #1001).
  2. Attach 1–3 clear photos of your pet.
  3. For a storybook, also tell us your pet's name, up to 3 family names and their favorite treat.

What makes a great photo

  • Bright, natural light (near a window is perfect)
  • Taken at your pet's eye level, face in focus
  • No filters, sunglasses or costumes covering the face
  • Send the original photo, not a screenshot

Within 24 hours we email you the artwork to approve. Nothing is made until you say yes.
