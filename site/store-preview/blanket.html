How it works

Four steps, one photo.

  1. Design it

    Upload a photo, pick a style, add names or a message.

  2. We polish it

    Our artist tidies the cut-out and checks it will weave well.

Woven, not printed

The picture is made of thread.

On a jacquard blanket the loom lifts colored cotton threads to build the image, so the portrait is part of the fabric, not ink on top of it.

Questions

Before you order.

What photo should I send?

A clear, well-lit photo of your pet's face with the eyes visible. Phone photos work well. Send 2–3 if you can.

Will it look exactly like my photo?

It's an illustration of your pet, not a photo. We keep their real markings, colors and expression in a bold style that weaves well, and you approve it before anything is made.

Is the art made with AI?

Yes. We use AI-assisted illustration, then check every image by hand against your photo and fix anything that's off.

Can you add my pet's name?

Yes. Add it in the designer above: up to 3 lines of text, placed and angled anywhere.

What if I don't like it?

You get up to 2 free art changes before production. If the finished blanket arrives damaged or doesn't match the approved art, we replace it free.

Can I return it?

Because it's made just for you, we can't take change-of-mind returns. Anything wrong with it gets a free replacement or a refund.

When will it arrive?

About 4–7 business days after you approve the art. Order by Dec 10 for Christmas delivery in the US.
