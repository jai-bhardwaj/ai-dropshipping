## Shipping policy

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to addresses in the United States (including APO/FPO where our carriers allow).

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within [PROCESSING DAYS] business days (Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays). Personalized items are made to order and are processed within [POD PROCESSING DAYS] business days after you approve your design, if approval is needed.

**Delivery time**
After processing, delivery usually takes [DELIVERY DAYS] business days. Your order may ship in more than one package if it contains items from different fulfillment centers.

**Shipping cost**
- Free standard shipping on orders over $[50]
- $[4.99] standard shipping on orders under $[50]

**Holiday deadline**
To receive your order before December 25, please order by **[DATE]** (standard shipping). Orders after this date are still shipped as fast as possible, but we can't guarantee delivery before Christmas.

**Tracking**
You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, email us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**Lost or stolen packages**
If tracking shows "delivered" but you can't find your package, please check with neighbors and your local post office, then contact us within 7 days. We'll work with the carrier to resolve it. If a package is lost in transit, we'll send a replacement or a full refund.

**Wrong address**
Please double-check your address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering. After the order ships, we can't change the address.

## Refund and return policy

**30-day returns (standard products)**
If you're not happy with a standard (non-personalized) product, you can request a return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

**How to return**
1. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your order number and the reason.
2. We'll reply within 1–2 business days with the return address and instructions. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.
3. Once we receive and check the item, we'll refund your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

**Return shipping**
Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or wrong.

**Personalized / custom items**
Because personalized items are made just for you, we can't accept returns for change of mind. If your personalized item arrives **damaged, defective, misprinted, or different from the approved design**, email us within 30 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund. **You won't need to send it back.**

**Damaged, defective or wrong items**
Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, at no cost to you.

**Cancellations**
Standard orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of ordering. Personalized orders can be cancelled until production starts (usually within [X] hours).

**Exchanges**
We don't offer direct exchanges; return the item for a refund and place a new order.
