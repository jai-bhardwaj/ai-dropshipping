# Pinterest plan: Woven Tails

Pinterest is search-driven and mostly US users. Pins keep getting traffic for months, unlike Reels. Goal: **5 pins/day** (fresh images, not repins).

## Setup
- Business account `@woventails`, claim `woventails.com`, connect the Shopify Pinterest app (product catalog → product pins).
- Profile name: **Woven Tails | Custom Pet Portrait Gifts**
- Bio: Your pet, illustrated from your photo, woven into a cotton blanket or starring in their own storybook. Made in the USA. 🐾

- 1000×1500 px (2:3), big title text on the top third, product in the middle, brand at the bottom.
- Link every pin to the matching product page (not the homepage).
