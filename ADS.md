# Meta ads plan: customer acquisition cost (CAC) + targeting, top 3 products

Date: 2026-10-08 · Market for ads: **United States first** (products ship from CJ's US warehouse) · Budget for paid tests: see section 5

## 1. The honest headline

- Across e-commerce on Meta in 2026, the **typical cost to get one purchase (CPA) is about $39** (Triple Whale median: CPM ~$15, purchase conversion ~1.5%).
- Our products make **about $20–26 per order before ads**.
- **So with "average" ads, we lose money on every first order.** To be profitable we need **better-than-average ads (CPA under ~$15–20)** and **bigger orders (bundles)**. The AI UGC hooks and bundle offers in this plan exist for exactly that reason.
- **Black Friday week:** ad prices (CPM) rise roughly 8–36% compared to October. Conversion rates also rise, so cost per order often holds steady, but don't assume it gets cheaper.

## 2. How CAC is calculated

```
CPA (cost per purchase) = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR × conversion rate)
```
- **CPM:** cost to show the ad 1,000 times
- **CTR:** % of viewers who click the link
- **Conversion rate:** % of store visitors who buy

| Scenario | CPM | Link CTR | Store conversion | **CPA** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bad ad / weak store | $18 | 1.0% | 1.0% | **$180** |
| Average (market median) | $15 | 1.5% | 1.5% | **~$67** (Triple Whale's blended median CPA is $39) |
| Good ad | $15 | 2.0% | 2.5% | **$30** |
| **Target (winning ad + bundles)** | $13 | 2.5% | 3.5% | **~$15** |

**Two levers decide everything:** the hook (CTR) and the product page + offer (conversion rate). That's why we test 3 hooks per product and add bundles.

**Indian advertiser note:** Meta charges **18% GST** on ad spend billed in India. $10 of ads costs you ~$11.80 (you can claim it back only if you're GST registered). The numbers below include this.

## 3. Break-even math per product

Fees: PayPal ~4.4% + $0.30 and Shopify's 2% third-party fee ≈ 6.5% + $0.30. Product costs are estimates until you send the real CJ prices.

| | Hand warmers | Mini photo printer | Motion-sensor lights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single price | $34.99 | $44.99 | $32.99 (3-pack) |
| Est. cost + US shipping | ~$11.50 | ~$16 | ~$10.50 |
| Fees | ~$2.60 | ~$3.20 | ~$2.45 |
| **Margin, single order** | **~$20.90** | **~$25.80** | **~$20.00** |
| Bundle offer | 2 sets: $59.99 | Printer + 3 paper rolls: $59.99 | 6-pack: $54.99 |
| **Margin, bundle** | **~$32.80** | **~$34.80** | **~$32.10** |
| Blended margin (if 30% pick bundle) | ~$24.50 | ~$28.50 | ~$23.60 |
| **Break-even CPA** (after 18% GST on ads) | **~$20.70** | **~$24.10** | **~$20.00** |
| **Target CPA** (≈ $8–10 profit per order) | **≤ $13** | **≤ $16** | **≤ $12** |
| Average order (blended) | ~$42.50 | ~$49.50 | ~$39.60 |
| Break-even ROAS | ~2.0 | ~2.0 | ~2.0 |
| Target ROAS | ≥ 3.2 | ≥ 3.1 | ≥ 3.3 |

ROAS = sales ÷ ad spend (Ads Manager shows it, before GST). Below break-even = losing money; at target = making money.

## 4. Who to target, per product

### How targeting works on Meta now (2026)
- Meta has merged/removed many interests (cut-off Jan 15, 2026) and now treats **interests (and age/gender, with Advantage+ Audience on) as suggestions**, not hard limits. Meta's AI decides who sees the ad.
- **The creative is the targeting.** A dog in the video finds dog owners; a Christmas stocking finds gift buyers. That's why each script shows the buyer's world.
- **What you control for sure:** country and minimum age. Everything else = hints.
- **Setup for a small budget:** **one campaign, one ad set, 3 ads**. Don't split $5/day across many audiences.

## 5. Budget: what ₹2,500 really buys

- ₹2,500 ≈ **$30**, or ~$25 of ads after 18% GST.
- At a realistic first-test CPA of $25–40, that's **about 1 sale**. **That's not enough to prove a product.** A fair test needs about **2–3× the break-even CPA per product** (~$50–60).

**So the ad plan is:**
1. **Free traffic first (weeks 2–4).** Organic Reels/Shorts/Pinterest pick the **one** product and **one** hook that work.
2. **Ads only on that one winner.** Start with the ₹2,500 + all organic profit.
3. **If you can add money:** about **₹5,000–8,000 more (~$60–90)** gives a real test of one product. That's the honest minimum.

### Test campaign setup (when we go paid)
| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sales |
| Conversion event | Purchase (Pixel + Conversions API set up and tested first) |
| Budget | $5–8/day, campaign level |
| Audience | Advantage+ Audience, US, min age per product, suggestions from section 4 |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements (mostly Reels/Stories feed for vertical video) |
| Ads | The 3 scripts (A/B/C) for the winning product |
| Don't touch | Don't edit the ad set for the first 3–4 days (learning phase) |

### Stop / keep / scale rules
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Ad spent $8 with link CTR under 1% | Stop the ad (hook isn't working) |
| Ad spent 1× break-even CPA (~$20), 0 add-to-carts | Stop the ad |
| Ad spent 2× break-even CPA (~$40), 0 purchases | Stop the ad |
| CPA under break-even for 3 days | Keep, make 3 new hooks with the same body |
| CPA under target for 3 days | Increase budget 20–30% every 2 days |
| CPA rises above break-even for 2 days after scaling | Go back to the last good budget |

## 6. What this means for the overall plan

- Paid ads go **US only** at first: products ship from CJ's US warehouse, so UK/CA/AU customers would wait longer and cost more to ship to. Add those countries once a supplier warehouse serves them.
- The **realistic profit per order is $5–10** at the start. 100 orders ≈ $500–1,000 profit, all reinvested.
- **The biggest wins come from:** (1) a hook with CTR above 2%, (2) bundles raising average order to ~$45+, (3) a product page that converts above 3%. I'll keep working on all three.
