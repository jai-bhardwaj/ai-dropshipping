## Refund policy

**Personalized items**
Every Woven Tails blanket and storybook is made to order from your pet's photo. That's why we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

**If something's wrong, we fix it**
If your item arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, or is different from the design you approved, email hello@woventails.com within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and a photo. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You won't need to send the item back.

**Cancellations**
You can cancel for a full refund until you approve your artwork proof. After approval, your item goes into production and can't be cancelled.

**Refund timing**
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Shipping policy

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to addresses in the United States, including APO/FPO addresses where our carriers allow.

**Shipping cost**
Free standard shipping on every US order.

**How long it takes**
- Art proof: we email it within 24 hours of your order. Reply to approve it or to ask for changes.
- Production: 1–3 business days after you approve. Every piece is made to order in the USA by our print partner.
- Delivery: usually 2–5 business days after production.

Gift sets ship together when possible. Occasionally the blanket and the book arrive in two packages.

**Christmas deadline**
Order by December 10 and approve your proof within 24 hours to get it by December 25. We ship later orders as fast as we can, but we can't guarantee delivery by Christmas.

**Tracking**
We email you a tracking number as soon as your order ships. If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, email hello@woventails.com.

**Lost or stolen packages**
If tracking shows delivered but you can't find your package, check with your neighbors and your local post office, then contact us within 7 days. If a package is lost in transit, we'll send a free replacement or a full refund.

**Wrong address**
Please double-check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering. We can't change the address once the order has shipped.
